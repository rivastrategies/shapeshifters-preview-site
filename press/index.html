Houston’s nine-piece classic-rock horn band. Trumpet, trombone, sax, flute, guitars, keys, drums, and a percussion battery — with most of the musicians singing.

Short bio

ShapeShifters of Houston is a nine-piece rock and classic-rock horn band formed in 1998. The group performs across the Houston and Galveston County area — clubs, hotel ballrooms, country clubs, festivals, and private rooms — and is available for corporate events, private parties, banquets, and galas. Instrumentation includes trumpet, trombone, saxophone, flute, electric and acoustic guitar, bass, keyboards, drums, congas, bongos, timbales, and additional percussion. Most members sing. They are a cover band performing the work of the original artists.

Entity

This is ShapeShifters of Houston, the Houston-area nine-piece classic-rock horn band established in 1998 — not the UK house act The Shapeshifters, not New Zealand drum-and-bass group Shapeshifter, and not the Los Angeles hip-hop collective The Shape Shifters.
